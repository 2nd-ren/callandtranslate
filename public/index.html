How it works

Brief it. Put them on speaker. Stay in control.

You are on the call the whole time. The app is the person who speaks their language and gets things done.

01

Tell it the job

What you need done, the facts they will ask for, and the questions you want answered.

02

Dial and speakerphone

Call the number yourself, put the phone on speaker, and start the agent in their language.

03

Follow along live

Read the transcript and a translation into your language. Jump in to correct or add a question.

04

Keep the record

The transcript is saved. A report covers what was said, what was done, what was captured, and what is still outstanding.

On the call

A natural conversation. Nothing missed.

They hear a fluent caller. You see every line in your language, and you can take the wheel at any moment.

Live transcript and translation

As they talk, you read what was said and what it means in your language — so you can follow, not just hope.

Interrupt in your language

Add a question, change the request, or correct a detail. The agent conveys it in their language and continues.

Missing facts go through you

If they ask for something that isn’t in the brief, the agent says it will check with you, asks you, then answers them. No guessing, no dead air that hides a problem.

Everyone can hear everyone

Speakerphone is the point. You, the agent, and the other person share the same call, which keeps the conversation honest.

Who it’s for

When you need something done, and you don’t speak their language.

New in a country, or on holiday

Hotels, pharmacies, landlords, clinics, town halls, restaurants, delayed luggage, a missed reservation — the calls that stall because you can’t do them yourself.

  • Book, change, cancel, collect, confirm
  • Ask the questions you actually need answered
  • Leave with a written record, not a blur of a call

Companies without a translator on hand

Suppliers, venues, local offices, customers, and partners who work in another language. You don’t need a staff interpreter for a 12-minute call.

  • Get a status, a quote, a booking, or a yes/no
  • Give the agent the file notes the other side will ask for
  • Share the report with whoever has to follow up
After you hang up

The call doesn’t vanish.

You keep the transcript and a report you can act on — including anything that still needs doing.

What was saidFull transcript, with translation into your language.
What was doneThe outcome: booked, confirmed, refused, or still pending.
What was capturedNames, numbers, prices, times, and anything else they gave you.
What’s outstandingCallbacks, documents to send, questions they couldn’t answer.
Why this, not an interpreter

Traditional translation is often the wrong tool.

Hiring someone takes setup you don’t have when the pharmacy closes in twenty minutes, or a supplier needs an answer today.

Typical interpreter booking

  • Schedule a person, often ahead of time
  • Brief them separately, then hope nothing was missed
  • Hard to jump in, hard to keep a usable record
  • Overkill for a short, practical call

Call & Translate

  • Write the job in your own words — goal, facts, and questions
  • On speaker with you, live, in their language
  • You see every line and can correct it on the spot
  • Transcript and report waiting when you hang up
